Data, progress & fair play

Local progress and browser data

                        

                            Many Offline Games stores coins, unlocked content, daily-reward data and game progress locally on this device/browser using browser storage, including local storage and cache storage. Clearing cookies/site data, browser storage or cache; using private browsing; resetting/removing the browser or app; changing browser profiles; or some device-cleaning tools can delete that local data. Locally deleted data may not be recoverable, and recovery cannot be guaranteed.
                        

                    

                    

                        
Keep proof of payment

                        

                            Keep your PayPal/payment receipt. A payment receipt and locally stored game data are separate things. If local browser data is removed, the site may no longer have the local information needed to display the same balance, unlocks or progress on that browser.
                        

                    

                    

                        
Security and fair play

Suspicious or abusive activity may trigger protective measures such as request blocking, rate limits, transaction review, or restriction of affected features where technically available. Security limits may change to protect the service.
                        

                    

                    

                        
Consumer rights

They do not remove any consumer rights or protections that cannot legally be excluded.
