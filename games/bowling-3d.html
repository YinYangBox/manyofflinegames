Rules

Knock down ten pins in each of ten frames. You get two balls per frame. Set your starting Position and Spin, then make each shot by dragging from the ball.

Drag downward and release to bowl. A longer pull gives more power. Pull left to aim right, or right to aim left. Direction and power come from that gesture and are not saved for the next shot. A tap or an upward drag does not launch the ball.

Strike (X): all ten pins with the first ball. Score 10 plus the pins from your next two balls. Spare (/): all ten with two balls. Score 10 plus your next ball. Otherwise, score the pins you knocked down.

In frame ten, a strike earns two extra balls and a spare earns one. A strike on an extra ball resets the rack; otherwise the remaining pins stay for your next ball. The maximum score is 300. Pending bonuses appear as a dash on the scorecard.

Easy: bumpers keep the ball on the lane, 2 credits per pin. Normal: open gutters, 4 credits per pin. Hard: stronger hook and heavier pins, 7 credits per pin.

Strikes and spares earn extra credits. Finish all ten frames for a completion bonus. Buy ball and lane skins with your credits; every skin has the same playing properties.
