How to play

Rules

Keep the ball alive

Move the paddle with the mouse, touch, A/D, or the arrow keys. Press Space, tap the arena, or use Launch to release a waiting ball.

Clear each level

Destroy every breakable brick. Armored bricks need several hits. Falling power-ups can expand the paddle, slow the balls, add multiball, or build a safety shield.

Earn credits

Every destroyed brick pays immediately. Faster difficulties are harder to control but multiply the credits earned from each brick.
