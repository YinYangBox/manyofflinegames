How to fish

There is no flashing “NOW” button. Watch the rod.

1

Cast and wait

Tap or click the water to cast. Once the float settles, patience matters. Small twitches can be false nibbles; setting the hook too early spooks the fish.

2

Read the real bite

A real bite pulls the float sharply under the surface and bends the rod tip. There is deliberately no text prompt. Press SET HOOK or tap the water before the reaction window closes.

3

Fight, don't just hold

After the hook, hold REEL to bring the fish closer. When the fish surges, tension climbs fast. Release REEL to protect the line, then continue when it calms down.

4

Rarity pays

Common fish are reliable money. Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary fish are progressively harder to find and land, but their credit payout rises dramatically.

↟

Workshop progression

Luck increases rare-fish odds. Reaction gives you more time to set the hook. Reel Power shortens difficult fights. Credit Bonus permanently increases every catch payout.
