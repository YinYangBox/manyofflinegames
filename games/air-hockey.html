Your striker stays in the lower half. Move into the puck to shoot; a quicker stroke hits harder.

Settings

Touch offset places the striker slightly above your finger so you can see the puck.

Rules

Score in the goal at the top of the table. Protect your goal at the bottom. The first player to score seven goals wins.

Move the mouse over your half to guide your striker. On a phone or tablet, touch and drag. Your striker follows smoothly and cannot cross the centre line. Strike through the puck to give it speed, or angle your stroke for a bank shot.

The player who conceded the last goal serves next. If the puck stops for five seconds, it is gently put back into play. The side rails and goalposts both rebound the puck.

Easy reacts more slowly and makes larger aiming mistakes. Normal anticipates rebounds. Hard reacts quickly, predicts the puck's path and aims for openings in your defence. The CPU follows the same collision and half-table restrictions.

Each goal earns 3 / 6 / 10 credits on Easy / Normal / Hard. A win adds 30 / 70 / 130 credits. Earned credits are kept if you lose or leave. Use them to buy strikers, pucks and tables. Skins never change the physics.

Escape pauses. The game also pauses when you leave the tab. With Touch offset enabled, your striker sits above your finger rather than underneath it.
