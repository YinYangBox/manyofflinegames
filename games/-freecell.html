Rules

FreeCell has no hidden cards.

Goal

Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, building each suit from Ace to King.

Tableau

Build downward in alternating colors. An empty column accepts any card or valid sequence.

Free cells

Each of the four cells holds exactly one card. Empty cells increase how many cards you can move together.

Supermoves

A valid sequence can move as one action when there are enough empty cells and columns to perform the move legally card by card.

Controls

Tap a card or sequence, then tap its destination. You can also drag. Double-tap a top card to send it to a foundation when legal.
