Rules

You run forward automatically. Change between three lanes to avoid obstacles and keep going as long as you can. There is no finish line.

Move: A/D or Left/Right. Jump: W, Up or Space. Slide: S or Down. On mobile, use the buttons or swipe left, right, up and down on the course. Escape pauses the run.

Yellow hurdles: jump over them or change lanes. Red walls: change lanes. Blue overhead gates: slide underneath. Open gaps: jump across or change lanes; a fall ends your run. Spinning arms: jump over the low arm or take another lane.

Easy gives you 3 lives, Normal 2, and Hard 1. Speed rises and new obstacles become more tightly spaced as time passes. The free lane changes more frequently too. After a hit, you have a brief recovery period, which shortens over time. Falling into a gap ends your run.

Each coin earns 1 / 2 / 3 credits on Easy / Normal / Hard. You also earn 5 / 10 / 15 credits per 100 metres. Every 200 metres, a checkpoint awards 10 / 20 / 30 credits. Checkpoints do not end the run.

Credits are kept even if you lose or leave. Spend them on character skins. Every character has the same hitbox, speed and jump. Best distances are recorded separately for each difficulty.
