How to play

Rules

Aim and shoot

Press the stopped ball and pull in the opposite direction of your shot. The farther you drag, the harder the hit. Release to shoot. Mouse and touch use the same controls.

The course

Finish five random holes. Walls and bumpers rebound the ball, sand slows it down, water returns it to the start of that stroke, and linked portals move it across the green.

Earn credits

Each completed hole pays credits. Fewer strokes mean a larger reward, and difficult courses pay more. Credits are saved immediately and buy ball and flag skins.
