How to play

BUILD Select a tower, then tap open ground. Red placement means the path or another tower is too close.
TWO CURRENCIES Gold energy buys towers and upgrades during a run. Credits are permanent and unlock maps and skins.
EARN CREDITS Every defeated enemy awards permanent credits immediately. Harder difficulties and dangerous enemy types pay more.
UPGRADE Select a placed tower to improve damage, range or fire rate. Each tower can reach level four.
TARGETING Switch a tower between First, Strong and Last targeting to handle runners, tanks and bosses.
SPECIALISTS Frost slows, Cannon explodes, Tesla chains, Rail pierces armor and Dart fires quickly.
WAVES Call the next wave when ready. A wave ends after every enemy is destroyed or escapes.
LIVES Escaped enemies damage the base. Reach zero lives and the defense falls.
CHECKPOINTS Your layout is saved between waves. Continuing restores the beginning of the current wave.
