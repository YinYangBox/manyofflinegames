Guide

How to play

1 · Move, never liftEvery block slides one grid cell at a time. Blocks cannot overlap or leave the 4×5 frame.
2 · The red square is the keyBring the 2×2 red block to columns 2–3 on the bottom row. Reaching that position solves the puzzle.
3 · Random does not mean untestedThe game builds the reachable graph from a solved arrangement. Because every candidate belongs to that graph it is solvable, and a breadth-first solver gives its exact shortest distance to any valid exit state.
4 · Hints know the shortest routeA hint chooses a neighboring position whose solver distance is exactly one move lower. Using hints lowers the credit reward, but never changes the puzzle.
