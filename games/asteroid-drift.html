Rules

Fly and fire

Rotate with A/D or the arrow keys, thrust with W or Arrow Up, and fire with Space. On mobile, hold the controls over the game field. Use Show Controls whenever you want to display or hide the touch pad. The arena wraps at every edge.

Stay alive

You have three shield charges. Asteroids split when destroyed, and enemy ships arrive as the run grows. A shield cell occasionally restores one charge.

Earn credits

Credits are based on whole seconds survived. Harder modes have more asteroids and enemy ships, but their survival multiplier pays much faster. Your reward is secured when the run ends.
