Rules

Klondike without the dusty Windows 95 manual.

Build the tableau

Stack cards downward in alternating colors: a black 9 can take a red 8. Only a King can move into an empty column. Face-up sequences move together.

Build the foundations

Move Aces to the four foundation piles, then build each suit upward from Ace to King. Double-tap or double-click a playable card to send it there automatically.

Use the stock

Draw 1 reveals one card. Draw 3 reveals up to three and only the top waste card is playable. When the stock empties, tap it again to recycle the waste.

Controls

Tap a card then tap its destination, or drag it directly. Undo reverses moves, Hint searches for a useful move, and Auto becomes available when the remaining finish is mechanical.
