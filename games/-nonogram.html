How to play

Every clue describes consecutive filled squares.

Read the numbers

A clue like 3 1 means a block of three filled squares, then at least one empty square, then one filled square.

Fill & mark

Use Fill for confirmed picture cells and X for confirmed empty cells. On desktop, right-click marks an X instantly.

Touch controls

Choose a tool, then tap or drag. A floating preview appears above your finger so the cell underneath is not hidden by your hand.

No guessing needed

Each included puzzle is built to be solved logically. Hints can reveal one correct square if you get stuck.
