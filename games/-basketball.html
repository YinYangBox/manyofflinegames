How to play

Mouse and touch are both first-class controls.

Swipe to shoot

Press the ball, drag upward in the direction you want and release. A longer swipe gives more power. On touch screens the hit area is intentionally larger than the visible ball, so your finger does not need pixel-perfect aim.

Scoring

Clean swishes score extra. Bank shots and long shots also add points. Free Throw gives 10 attempts, Time Attack gives 60 seconds, and Streak ends after three misses.

Credits

Credits are awarded only when a session ends. Higher difficulties pay more, so restarting cannot be used to farm rewards.
