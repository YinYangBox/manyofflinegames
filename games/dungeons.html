Rules

Your expedition

Explore a connected, randomly generated dungeon. Destroy every monster spawner, then defeat every remaining monster to win. Spawners keep summoning while they stand, up to the living-enemy cap. You cannot win by only killing their summons.

Move & fight

WASD or arrow keys move. Aim with the mouse; click or hold Space to swing. Without mouse aim, attacks face your movement. E opens nearby chests or picks up loot. Touch controls provide movement, Attack, and Use; touch attacks aim at the nearest visible target in reach. Attacks never pass through walls.

Light & enemies

One tile represents one metre. Easy visibility is 7m, Normal 6m, Hard 4m, and Insane 3m. Walls block sight for you and the undead, including diagonal corners. The map remembers explored terrain, not unseen enemies. Skeletons fire arrows on EVERY difficulty. They aim during their warning flash, then release a straight shot: move sideways or take cover. Arrows stop at walls. Zombies attack in melee. Enemies only track your last seen position briefly.

Combat difficulty

Easy / Normal / Hard / Insane: zombie health is 58 / 84 / 144 / 240; skeleton health is 42 / 60 / 96 / 144. Zombie hits deal 7 / 13 / 26 / 38 damage and arrows deal 6 / 11 / 22 / 30 before armor. Higher modes also increase movement and attack speed. Your sword still deals 24 damage.

Spawner pressure

Spawner health is 120 / 288 / 720 / 1440 from Easy to Insane. They attempt waves of 1 / 1 / 2 / 3 monsters every 20 / 12 / 7 / 4.5 seconds, with global limits of 18 / 32 / 55 / 80 living monsters. Crowded spawn tiles can delay a summon. Destroy spawners to stop new waves; then clear the remaining undead.

Inventory & armor

Tab or Inventory pauses the expedition. Your backpack has exactly five slots. Helmet, chestplate, gloves, and boots each have a separate equipment slot. Select armor to equip or swap it; select a potion to drink it. The sword is your permanent weapon and needs no backpack slot. Unequipping needs a free slot. Dropped items remain on the floor.

Coffers & credits

Enemies pay 12 / 18 / 26 / 36 credits by difficulty. Credit chests contain 55–100 credits multiplied by 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3. Other chests hold armor or a potion instead. A full backpack never deletes loot: it stays on the ground. Skin purchases are permanent and only available outside combat.

Survival & saving

You start with 100 health and two potions. Armor reduces incoming damage, up to 60%. Drinking a potion restores 40 health. Death ends the expedition and loses its equipment, but earned credits and skins stay. Inventory and Pause stop all monsters. Menu, hiding the tab, and periodic checkpoints save the run. Offline play needs no downloaded assets; progress requires browser storage.
