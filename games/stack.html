Rules

  1. Place the moving block. It alternates between two directions. The part outside the block below breaks away and falls. Miss completely and the run ends.
  2. Perfect fits build a combo. Land inside the small perfect-fit tolerance to keep the whole block. Any imperfect placement breaks the streak.
  3. Combo 5 starts regrowth. Every perfect fit from the fifth onward adds 0.14 units to both sides, up to the original size. Keep the streak alive to recover lost area.
  4. Survive for credits. Every 4 active seconds earns 1 / 2 / 3 credits on Easy / Normal / Hard. Perfect fits award 2 × combo × difficulty multiplier extra credits, up to combo 25 for rewards.
  5. Keep moving. You have 12 / 10 / 8 seconds to place each block. Pauses earn nothing. Credits are saved as you earn them, even if you lose.
  6. Skins are cosmetic. Panels, crystals, and ornaments do not change collision sizes. Speed rises gradually with tower height.
