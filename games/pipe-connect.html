Rules

Rotate the pipes to make a continuous route from the blue IN port on the left to the golden OUT port on the right. Tap or click a tile to rotate it clockwise. Right-click to rotate counterclockwise. On a keyboard, move the selection with the arrow keys and press Space to turn it; hold Shift to turn back.

Press Run water to test your route. Water can move between two neighbouring pipes only when their openings face each other. An open end, a mismatched connection or the wrong edge stops the flow. The stopping point is marked in red. Turn the pieces and try again: failed tests do not end the puzzle.

You do not need to use every pipe. Unused pieces may have open ends. Every generated puzzle has a solution, and any valid route to OUT is accepted.

Easy uses a 4 × 4 board, Normal 6 × 6, Hard 8 × 8 and Expert 10 × 10. Larger boards have longer routes and more distracting pipes. There is no time limit and taking your time does not reduce your reward.

Completing a puzzle gives 20 / 40 / 70 / 110 credits by difficulty. Earn up to 50% extra for efficient rotation. A clean solve without hints or failed water tests adds another 25%. Undo restores a rotation but does not remove its cost from your turn count.

A hint highlights and correctly turns one piece on a known solution route. Hints are free; each reduces the efficiency bonus by 10 percentage points and removes the clean-solve bonus. You always keep the full base reward.

Use credits in Skins to unlock complete pipe themes. They change the pipes, fittings and details, never the rules. Your current puzzle and purchases save automatically. Time pauses while you are in a menu or another tab.
