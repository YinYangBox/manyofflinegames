Rules

Simple rules, deep decisions.

1

Trap the opponent

Place a disc so that one or more enemy discs are trapped in a straight horizontal, vertical or diagonal line between your new disc and another disc of your color. Every trapped disc flips to your color.

2

Only legal moves count

A move must flip at least one enemy disc. The small dots on the board show every legal square for the current player, so you never have to guess where a move is allowed.

3

Passes happen automatically

If you have no legal move, your turn is passed. The match ends when neither player can move or when the board is full. Whoever owns more discs wins.

4

Corners are powerful

Corner discs can never be flipped. Taking a corner is usually strong; giving your opponent an easy corner is usually dangerous. Edges and mobility matter more than simply having the most discs early.

⌁

Controls

Mouse / touch: click or tap any highlighted legal square. Undo: takes back the last decision; against the bot it returns you to your previous turn. Hint: highlights a strong move, but hints are limited on bot difficulties.
