The operating manual

Guide

Simple controls. A connected economy.

1. Produce and sell

The free Timber Yard starts immediately. Each finished cycle creates real stock. Downstream factories consume that stock before auto-export runs. Revenue comes from exports, manual sales, and delivered orders.

2. Earn credits

Early achievements grant your first credits. You also earn credits by completing a prestige reset. Revenue is a reset score, not a second purchasing currency. Factories, lines, upgrades, and research are bought with credits.

3. Build a chain

Factories require upstream sites. A missing ingredient pauses a cycle without consuming partial inputs. Keep some raw materials in reserve rather than selling everything. Pausing a factory also removes its power demand.

4. Begin again, stronger

Prestige keeps every permanent purchase and your spending balance. Your campaign revenue, stored goods, active orders, and production progress reset. Lifetime credits earned improve production even after you spend them.

Production chains

Power, capacity, and bottlenecks

The grid provides 14 power initially. If enabled lines demand more, all production slows in proportion to available power. Research Grid Engineering to expand supply. Each product has its own capacity; full storage stops that factory. Warehouse Expansion and local Storage upgrades add room. Reserve settings affect sales only, so downstream factories may always consume those goods.

How orders and prices work

Contracts quote their payout when generated. Market shifts affect future sales and new quotes, never an accepted quote. Contracts expire after ten minutes of production time. Delivering consumes the listed goods and adds the quoted revenue. Skipping has no currency penalty, but a 60-second cooldown prevents instant rerolling.

Offline work and time limits

Closing the game simulates up to 8 hours of production when you return, extended by Offline Logistics to 24 hours. The simulation uses the same one-second steps, inputs, reserves, power limits, and markets as live play. Orders can expire offline, but are never automatically delivered. Production must be enabled. Clock rollback awards no extra time. Keep one active tab per save.

Prestige calculation

Reset credits equal floor((campaign revenue ÷ 1,200)^0.65 × (1 + 0.06 × Legacy Accounting level)). Reset becomes available at one credit. The preview shows exactly what you will receive. Early milestone credits are awarded once per save, so repeating a new campaign cannot farm them.

Overclock and research

Overclock doubles production for 20 seconds, followed by an 80-second recovery. Research extends its duration and shortens recovery. It also increases demand while active, so adequate power matters. The button works with mouse, touch, or Enter while focused. No tapping is required for normal production.

Saving and backups

Progress is saved automatically and after purchases or deliveries. If storage is unavailable, a warning appears and you can export a JSON backup. Imported backups are validated before replacing the game. All save data is contained in one Idle Factory object in your arcade's shared save.
