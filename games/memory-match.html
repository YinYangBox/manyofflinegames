How to play

Rules

The timer is generous. Bigger boards are always worth more.

1

Build your board

Choose any owned designs. Five pairs is the minimum and there is no smaller board.

2

Find every match

Turn over two cards. Matching designs stay visible; different cards turn back over.

3

Take your time

The clock starts on your first card. The time penalty begins only after a generous grace period.

4

Earn more with more pairs

Every extra pair raises the base reward. The time penalty is slow and can never remove more than 30%.
